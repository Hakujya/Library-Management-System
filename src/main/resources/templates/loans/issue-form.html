Circulation Rules
  • Inventory Decrement: Available copies of the selected book will automatically decrease by 1 upon confirmation.
  • Active Status Verification: Books can only be issued to members whose account status is currently Active.
  • Overdue Tracking: Loans past their due date will automatically flag on the dashboard and in overdue reports.
  • Return Processing: Returning a book immediately restores +1 copy to the available catalog count.
